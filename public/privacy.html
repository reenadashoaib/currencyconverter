Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

What RateNote stores

RateNote has no accounts and doesn’t ask for your name or email. The converter saves the latest exchange rates in your browser’s local storage so the page loads faster and works offline. That data stays on your device.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads from Google AdSense and other advertising partners. These partners use cookies and similar technologies to show ads and to measure how ads perform.

  • Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.
  • Google’s advertising cookies let it and its partners serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalised advertising in Google Ad Settings, or opt out of some third-party vendors at aboutads.info.
  • Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

Exchange-rate services

To load rates, your browser contacts ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com) and, if needed, Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). Like any website, these services receive your IP address with each request.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes request data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect the site.
